I am a Ph.D. student in Control and Automation at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi). My research focuses on adaptive control, distributed estimation, and multi-agent robotic systems, combining theoretical analysis with experimental validation using networks of quadrotor UAVs.

My Ph.D. research focuses on adaptive coverage control for mobile sensor networks, with particular emphasis on distributed estimation and relaxed excitation conditions. My initial work was based on a parameterized model of the unknown spatial distribution, with exponential convergence guarantees, and was later extended to a more general setting by exploring function convergence guarantees in an infinite-dimensional space such as a reproducing kernel Hilbert space (RKHS). My work involves both theoretical development and experimental validation through real-time experiments on a network of quadrotor UAVs using an OptiTrack motion capture system.


## Research Interests

My current research interest includes:

- Adaptive Control, Distributed Estimation
- Learning-Based Control
- Optimization
- Multi-Agent Systems, Autonomous UAVs
